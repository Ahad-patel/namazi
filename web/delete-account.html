Delete your Masjid Timer account

In the app or on the website

  1. Sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE, and confirm.

Without the app

Email contact@masjid-timer.invalid from the address you signed in with, asking us to delete your account. We do it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address and display name.
  • Your votes.
  • Your masjid admin claims, including phone numbers and proof photos.

What stays

  • Jamat times you suggested stay in the app without your name, so other people can still see them.
  • Masjids you added stay listed, without your name.

Deletion can't be undone. You can download your data first from Settings → Download my data.

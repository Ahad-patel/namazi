Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-01 · Masjid Timer (Android, iOS and web)

Masjid Timer shows nearby masjids and their jamat times. You can browse without an account. This policy explains what we collect when you do sign in, why, and what you can do about it. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and is compatible with the GDPR.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and display name (from Google sign-in or the email code), the date you accepted these terms, and that you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • What you contribute: jamat and azaan times you suggest, notes on them, your votes, and masjids you add.
  • Masjid admin claims: your role at the masjid, a phone number, and an optional proof photo. Only the reviewer (the app owner) sees the phone number and photo.

What we don't collect

  • Your location is not stored. When you use "near me", your phone's location is sent once with the search to find nearby masjids. It is never saved to our database or written to our logs.
  • No contacts, no birth date, no gender, no advertising ID. No ads, no analytics trackers, no marketing email.
  • Your data is not sold, and it is not used to train AI.

Why we use it

  • Email: to sign you in. We only send sign-in codes, never marketing.
  • Display name: shown next to your suggestions to the reviewer when moderating.
  • Phone and proof photo: to check that you really help run the masjid you claimed, usually with a phone call.

How long we keep it

  • Claim phone numbers and proof photos: deleted 90 days after the claim is approved or rejected.
  • Suggestions that were replaced by a newer one: deleted after 1 year.
  • Your account: until you delete it. If you are banned from contributing, the record of the ban is kept while the ban stands.

Your rights

  • Access: Settings → Download my data gives you everything we hold about you as a JSON file.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete my account. Your profile, votes and claims (with photos) are deleted. Times you suggested stay in the app without your name, so the community data keeps working. See account deletion.
  • Correction, grievance, or any question: email contact@masjid-timer.invalid. This is also our grievance contact under the DPDP Act. We reply within 30 days.

Children

Browsing needs no account. Creating an account requires you to confirm you are 18 or older. The app is not designed for children.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), hosted on AWS in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1).
  • Vercel (hosts this website).
  • MapTiler (map tiles and area search). Map requests send your IP address and the map area you are viewing to MapTiler.
  • Google (only if you choose "Continue with Google").
  • Sentry (crash reports from the production app, with personal data switched off; no location or email is sent).
  • Our email provider (sends the sign-in code email).

Masjid locations come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).

Cookies and storage

The website stores your sign-in session and your theme choice in your browser. Nothing is used for tracking, so there is no cookie banner.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app asks you to accept the new version before you contribute again.

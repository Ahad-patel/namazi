What the app is

Masjid Timer lists masjids and their jamat times. Times are either set by an approved admin of that masjid ("Verified") or suggested by the community ("Community"). The app is free.

Times can change

We do not guarantee that any time is correct or up to date. Masjids change their times, and community suggestions can be wrong. Always confirm with the masjid. We are not responsible for a missed jamat.

Your account

  • You must be 18 or older to create an account. Browsing needs no account.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. You are responsible for what is posted from your account.

Community rules

  • Only suggest times you believe are correct for that masjid.
  • No abuse, spam, advertising, or personal information in notes or masjid names.
  • Only claim a masjid you actually help run (imam, mutawalli or committee member).
  • Don't try to get around limits (for example, 10 suggestions a day) or access other people's data.

Moderation

We may hide suggestions or masjids, reject or revoke admin claims, and stop an account from contributing, at our discretion. Hidden content can be restored. Every moderation action is logged.
